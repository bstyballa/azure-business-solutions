# 01. Azure Cost Visibility & Automated Alerting System

## 📌 Business Problem
Small and mid-sized businesses migrating to the cloud often experience "cloud bill shock" due to unmonitored resource growth and lack of financial visibility, leading to unpredictable monthly operational expenses.

## 🛠️ Technical Solution & Architecture
* **Azure Cost Management:** Tracks spending across resources and translates technical resource charges into business-readable spend categories.
* **Azure Monitor & Logic Apps:** Automatically triggers notification workflows via email when spend hits predefined budget thresholds ($50, $100, $200).
* **Azure Workbooks:** Provides an executive dashboard breaking down spend trends week-over-week.

## 🎯 Business Outcome
Gives business owners real-time visibility into cloud operational costs and delivers automated alerts before budget overruns occur.
